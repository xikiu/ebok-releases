# Sample ebook

[Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland](alice-in-wonderland.epub), by Lewis Carroll, is included so you can try Ebok without supplying a book of your own.

Source: [Project Gutenberg, ebook 11](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/11). Project Gutenberg lists this work as **public domain in the USA**. The EPUB is unmodified and retains the original credits and full Project Gutenberg license inside the book. Public-domain status depends on your country; the included Gutenberg notice explains this.

[Download the sample EPUB](https://github.com/xikiu/ebok-releases/releases/download/v0.1.0-alpha.2/alice-in-wonderland.epub), open Ebok, and choose **Import books** (or drag the EPUB into the library).
